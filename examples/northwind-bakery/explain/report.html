Why search traffic fell in October

Northwind Bakery · 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-25 compared with 2026-08-31 to 2026-09-27 · Prepared by Readout example

Clicks from Google search fell 38.5% because three sourdough blog posts dropped from about position 4 to about position 7. The drop started on October 6, the day after the new blog template launched.

Search Console clicks went from 16,971 to 10,434 between the two 28-day periods. That is far outside the normal range for this site, and it is not a seasonal pattern: over the same weeks last year, clicks moved only -6.7%.

Almost all of the loss sits in three blog posts about sourdough, and it comes from lower rankings rather than fewer searches. Branded searches for Northwind Bakery held up.

The timing points first to the blog redesign on October 5. It also falls inside Google's September 2026 spam update, so both are worth checking. Timing alone does not prove either one caused it.

1. Is the change real?

Probably real

Yes. The drop shows up in both Search Console and GA4, and the ratio between the two barely moved (0.859 to 0.826), so this is a real change in visits, not a tracking problem.

Separately, GA4 recorded only 20 sessions on October 12, against a usual level of about 854. Every channel dropped that day, which points to a tracking gap, not lost customers. It does not affect the Search Console numbers in this report, but GA4 totals for this period are slightly understated.

4. What kind of change is it?

Ranking loss

This is a ranking loss. Average position across the site slipped from 5.7 to 7.3, and on the sourdough starter guide it went from 4.0 to 7.1. Lower positions mean fewer people see and click the result, so the click-through rate fell from 9.47% to 6.66%.

It is not a brand problem: branded clicks moved -7.6% while non-branded clicks fell 49.2%.

5. When did it start?

The trend shifted on October 6. Daily clicks averaged 630.9 before and 292.8 after, a -53.6% shift with a strong signal.

That date coincides with the blog redesign launched on October 5 and falls inside Google's September 2026 spam update (September 24 to October 8).

6. What to check next

Start with the redesign, since it touched exactly the pages that lost rankings. Check the new blog template for changes to titles, headings, internal links, canonical tags and noindex settings. Then compare the three posts with the pages that now rank above them.
